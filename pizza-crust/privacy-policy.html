Pizza Crust Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Pizza Crust (Android package com.littleslice.pizzatycoon), published by Moon Games, handles data.

Local game progress

Pizza Crust stores coins, locations, upgrades, outfits, achievements, settings and other game progress locally in the app's private storage on your device. Moon Games does not receive this save data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob for app-open and interstitial ads and for optional rewarded videos. It uses Google's User Messaging Platform to display consent choices where required. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and device or app identifiers for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Pizza Crust requests contextual, non-personalized ads and removes Android Advertising ID, Topics and attribution permissions from its manifest.

Ad data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure. Network data is encrypted in transit. If Google requires a privacy-options entry point for your region, Pizza Crust shows Manage ad privacy choices in Settings.

Your choices

Rewarded videos are optional. Declining them does not block normal play. Core game progress works offline. You can revisit available ad privacy choices in Settings and can delete all local progress by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Other services

Pizza Crust has no account system, login, analytics, telemetry, backend, cloud save or remote configuration. Moon Games does not sell personal information.
