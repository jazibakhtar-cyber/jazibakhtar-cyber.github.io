World Runner Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

This policy explains how World Runner (Android package com.worldrunner.game), published by Moon Games, handles data.

Game progress

World Runner stores coins, high score, selected and unlocked skins and themes, settings, tutorial status, daily rewards, missions, and upgrades locally on your device. Moon Games does not receive this save data. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and advertising partners: IP address (used to estimate approximate location), app interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostics such as launch time, hangs and energy use, and device or account identifiers such as Android advertising ID and app set ID where available.

These data are used for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Google states that Mobile Ads data is encrypted in transit with TLS. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children and families

World Runner is listed for ages 9 and older. All ad requests use child-directed treatment and G-rated ad content because the game has no age gate. Interest-based advertising and remarketing are disabled for these requests. The game does not request names, email, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.

Your choices

Where required, Settings provides Ad Privacy Choices. You can reset or delete Android's advertising ID in device privacy settings. Rewarded ads only load after you press the free-coins button.

Sharing and accounts

There is no World Runner account system or game-data server, and Moon Games does not sell personal data. Advertising data is transmitted to Google and its advertising partners as described above.

Retention and deletion

Clear World Runner's app data or uninstall the game to delete local progress. Moon Games keeps no server copy of that progress. Google controls retention of AdMob data under Google's policies; use Google's privacy controls for requests about that data.

Security

Advertising data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS supplied by Google. No electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Moon Games support page or the developer contact shown on World Runner's Google Play listing.
